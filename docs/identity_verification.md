# Identity Verification (KYC)

Acme Markets is required by anti-money-laundering (AML) regulations to verify the identity of every customer.
Verification must be completed before the first withdrawal, or once cumulative deposits exceed 2,000 USD,
whichever happens first.

## Accepted documents

Proof of identity: a valid passport, national identity card, or driving licence. The document must not be
expired, must be in colour, and all four corners must be visible.

Proof of address: a utility bill, bank statement, or government-issued letter showing the customer's full
name and residential address, dated within the last 6 months. Mobile phone bills and screenshots are not
accepted as proof of address.

## Submitting documents

Customers upload documents from Settings > Verification in the web or mobile app. The upload also asks for a
selfie with a liveness check, which is compared against the photo on the identity document. Accepted file
formats are JPG, PNG and PDF, up to 10 MB per file.

## Review times

Most verification submissions are reviewed within 24 hours. During peak periods the review can take up to
3 business days. Customers receive an email when the review is complete, whether it is approved or rejected.

## Rejected submissions

Common reasons for rejection are blurry or cropped images, expired documents, and a name that does not match
the account. Customers may resubmit after a rejection. After 3 rejected submissions, the case is escalated
to the compliance team for manual review and the customer is contacted within 2 business days.

## Data retention

Verification documents are stored encrypted and are retained for 5 years after the account is closed, as
required by AML record-keeping rules. Customers cannot request early deletion of verification records.

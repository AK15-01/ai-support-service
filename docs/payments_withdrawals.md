# Payments and Withdrawals

This guide covers deposit methods, withdrawal rules, processing times, and fees for Acme Markets accounts.

## Supported payment methods

Customers can deposit and withdraw using bank transfer (SEPA and SWIFT), debit or credit card (Visa and
Mastercard), and e-wallets (Skrill and Neteller). Third-party payments are not accepted: the payment method
must be in the account holder's own name.

## Withdrawal rules

Withdrawals are available only after identity verification is complete. Funds are returned to the method used
for the original deposit, up to the deposited amount; any profit above that amount is paid by bank transfer.
The minimum withdrawal amount is 10 USD. Verified accounts can withdraw up to 50,000 USD per day.

## Processing times

| Method | Internal processing | Typical arrival |
|--------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Bank transfer | 1 business day | 3-5 business days |
| Card | 1 business day | Up to 10 business days |
| E-wallet | Same business day | Within 24 hours |

## Fees

Deposits are free. The first withdrawal in each calendar month is free; each additional withdrawal in the same
month costs 2.50 USD. SWIFT transfers may also incur intermediary bank charges that Acme Markets does not
control.

## Pending and failed withdrawals

A withdrawal can be cancelled while its status is Pending. Once the status changes to Processing, it can no
longer be cancelled. If a withdrawal fails, for example because the bank rejects it, the funds are returned to
the account balance within 2 business days and the customer is notified by email.
